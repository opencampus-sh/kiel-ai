# Week 10 – Project Presentation

**Estimated workload:** about 11.8 hours (session 1.75 h, presentation and repository 10 h)

**Question of the week (from Anna):** _What should Anna do differently on Monday morning?_

**Canvas focus:** block 11 (Recommendation) · **Skills:** `/ds-retro`

### This week you will ...

* present your results to a decision-maker clearly within 8–10 minutes, with every team member contributing,
* answer questions from a non-technical audience honestly, including the limits of your forecast,
* document your project reproducibly for a technical audience.

### Your presentation

Prepare an **8 to 10-minute presentation** for Anna. Every team member presents a part. It includes:

* your team members’ names on the title slide,
* a list and short description of the variables you created,
* bar charts with confidence intervals for two of these variables,
* your linear model: model equation and adjusted R²,
* the missing value imputation you used,
* your neural network: the source code that defines it, loss curves for training and validation data, and the MAPE overall and per product group,
* your “worst fail” and “best improvement”, including mistakes the coding agent made and how you caught them.
