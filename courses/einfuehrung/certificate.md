# Certificate and Project Submission

All participants are expected to work towards a certificate of achievement or ECTS credits. The course is worth **5 ECTS**, about 150 hours of work in total.

### Requirements for a certificate or ECTS

* [x] Attendance at at least 80 % of the sessions (you may miss at most two sessions).
* [x] Completion of the weekly tasks.
* [x] Presentation of your team project in the final session; every team member presents a part (see [week 10](week-10-presentation.md)).
* [x] Project submitted and approved on [EduHub](https://edu.opencampus.sh), with a public GitHub repository to which all team members have contributed.

#### Online attendance

If you attend online, please use your full name, the same one you used to register at [edu.opencampus.sh](https://edu.opencampus.sh). **Otherwise your attendance will not be recorded!**

{% hint style="info" %}
Online attendance is only accredited if you have your camera on, participate with a laptop or desktop computer, and are in a sufficiently quiet place to take part in the group work.
{% endhint %}

### Your team project on EduHub

From week 3 you work in a team of three. EduHub manages each team project as one project with several authors. You can be an accepted author on only one active project per course.

| Step | Who | What to do on EduHub | Week |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Use the template | One team member | Click “Use this template” on the course’s project template. EduHub creates your team project with you as the first author. | 3 |
| Join | Other team members | Click “Request to join” on your team’s project. The first author accepts the requests via “Manage requests”. | 3 |
| Request review | First author | Once all requests are decided, switch off “Accepting participants” and click “Request review”. | 3 |
| Team confirmed | Instructor | Confirms your team. From now on the team is fixed. | 3–4 |
| Prepare deliverables | All authors | Fill in the “My Project” panel. A checklist shows which items are still missing. | 9–10 |

{% hint style="warning" %}
Join requests are blocked once the submission deadline has passed, so make sure your team is complete on EduHub well before the end of the course.
{% endhint %}

### Submitting your project

When the checklist in the “My Project” panel is complete, **one team member** clicks “Submit for review” on behalf of the team. After that the project is read-only for the team. The instructors then approve it, send it back for revision, or reject it.

Your submission contains four items:

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Documentation (PDF, DOCX, ODT or ZIP, max. 23 MB) | Your project report, e.g. the main README exported as PDF: summary; team, roles and individual contributions; data sources and self-created variables; bar charts with confidence intervals; linear model (equation, adjusted R²); imputation method; neural network (architecture, loss curves, MAPE overall and per product group); worst fail and best improvement; a reflection, including how you used the coding agent and how you verified its results. |
| Presentation (PDF, PPT/PPTX or ODP, max. 23 MB) | The slides of your final presentation. |
| External link | Your public GitHub repository with data, documented code and READMEs. |
| Cover image | A key visualization of your project, shown on the project tile. |

{% hint style="danger" %}
Documentation and presentation can be downloaded publicly once uploaded. They must not contain passwords, API keys or personal data.
{% endhint %}
